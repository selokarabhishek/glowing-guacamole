Frequently asked questions

How does it work?

FormFill AI uses computer vision to detect form fields, then auto-fills them with your saved data. It learns from each form you fill to get smarter over time.

Is my data secure?

Yes. Your data is stored locally on your computer. For sensitive forms, use the local Ollama option which keeps everything on your machine. We never see your data.

What forms does it work with?

Any web form - job applications, contact forms, checkout pages, CRM systems, and more. If it's a form on the web, FormFill AI can handle it.

Do I need to configure anything?

No. Install the extension and it works immediately. No API keys, no setup, no configuration needed.

Can I use it for my team?

Yes! The Team plan lets you share form templates across your organization. Perfect for sales, recruiting, and ops teams.

What's your refund policy?

30-day money-back guarantee, no questions asked. If you're not saving time, we'll refund you immediately.
